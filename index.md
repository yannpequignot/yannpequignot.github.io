> As a mathematician and machine learning researcher, I am constantly inspired by the foundational work of logicians and mathematicians who formalized the concept of computer program less than a century ago. 
>This fills me with both humility and enthusiasm!


Since July 2020, I am the scientific coordinator of the [DEEL project](https://deel.quebec/) and a member of the [IID](https://iid.ulaval.ca/) at Laval University.

I obtained my PhD in Mathematics in 2015 from the University Paris-Diderot (joint program with the University of Lausanne, Switzerland) for my dissertation "Better-quasi-order: Ideals and spaces" ([Preamble](Pdf/PhD-Thesis-YPequignot-preamble.pdf)), which deals with combinatorics, order theory, and descriptive set theory.

I joined Laval University, Québec, in November 2019 at the [Centre de recherche en données massives](https://crdm.ulaval.ca/) as a postdoctoral scholar under the supervision of François Laviolette. Before that, I held a postdoctoral position at the [Department of Mathematics and Statistics](https://secureweb.mcgill.ca/mathstat/) of [McGill University](https://mcgill.ca/) in Montreal from September 2018 to June 2019,. From January 2017 to September 2018, I was an Early Postdoc.Mobility fellow of the [Swiss National Science Foundation](http://www.snf.ch/en/Pages/default.aspx) at the [Department of Mathematics](http://www.math.ucla.edu/) of the [University of California Los Angeles](http://www.ucla.edu/). In 2016, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Kurt Gödel Research Center](http://www.logic.univie.ac.at/Home.html). 


### My research interests

- Machine Learning: Explainability, Robustness, and Trustworthiness
- Descriptive set theory, Infinite games with perfect information
- Combinatorics, Well-quasi-order, and better-quasi-order
- Computability, Effective Descriptive Set Theory, Computable analysis
